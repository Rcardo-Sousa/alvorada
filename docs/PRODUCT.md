# PRODUCT.md — Alvorada

## What it is
Alvorada is a wedding ceremony and creative direction studio based in Cuiabá, Mato Grosso. The site is a cinematic brand experience: a scroll-driven drone arrival into the ceremony grounds, then a continuous editorial story that sells intention, calm planning, and authored aesthetics.

## Audience
Couples planning a wedding who want something personal rather than formulaic — people who care about light, place, pacing, and memory. Secondary: planners and collaborators looking for Alvorada’s sensibility.

## Visitor job (primary surface)
**Persuade + Experience.** The visitor should feel the brand’s world, then want to start a conversation. The artifact (images, tone, motion) leads; the UI recedes.

## Voice
Warm, precise, unhurried. Portuguese with editorial restraint. Prefer concrete sensory language over wedding clichés. Soft confidence, never salesy.

## Constraints
- Vanilla HTML/CSS/ES modules (no framework rebuild).
- Preserve the drone→editorial handoff narrative.
- Content must remain visible by default (no opacity:0 gates that can stick).
- Light/dark themes already exist; refinements must work in both.
- Do not invent client claims, prices, or fake case studies.

## Evidence of success
Visitor reaches `#contato` feeling oriented and emotionally primed; primary CTA “Vamos conversar” is obvious without shouting.
